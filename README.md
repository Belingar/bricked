# 🧱 Bricked

Play here 👉 https://belingar.github.io/bricked/

## 🎮 About the Game
**Bricked** is a classic arcade-style brick breaker game.  
Your goal is to destroy all the bricks using a bouncing ball while keeping it in play with your paddle.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

- ⬅️ **Left Arrow** — Move paddle left  
- ➡️ **Right Arrow** — Move paddle right  
- 🖱️ *(Optional)* Move mouse — Control paddle (if enabled)  
- ⏎ **Space / Click** — Launch the ball (if your game uses a start input)

---

## 🧠 How to Play

- Use the paddle to bounce the ball.
- The ball will break bricks when it hits them.
- Don’t let the ball fall below the paddle!
- Aim carefully to hit multiple bricks and clear levels faster.

---

## 🏆 How to Win

- 🎯 Destroy **all the bricks** on the screen to complete the level.
- 🚀 Progress through levels (if your game has multiple stages).
- 💯 Try to get the highest score possible!

---

## ❌ Game Over

- The game ends if you **miss the ball** and it falls off the screen.

---

## 💡 Tips

- Aim for the top rows to keep the ball bouncing longer.
- Try to hit clusters of bricks for faster clearing.
- Stay focused as the ball speeds up!
