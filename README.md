# 🧱 Bricked

Play here 👉 https://belingar.github.io/bricked/

## 🎮 About the Game
**Bricked** is a classic arcade-style brick breaker game.  
Your goal is to destroy all the bricks using a ball while avoiding the falling tanks.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

- ⬅️ **Left Arrow** — Move paddle left  
- ➡️ **Right Arrow** — Move paddle right  
- ⏎ **Space** — Launch the ball

---

## 🧠 How to Play

- Use the cannon to shoot the ball.
- The ball will break tanks when it hits them.
- Don’t let the tanks fall on you!
- Aim carefully to hit multiple bricks and clear levels faster.

---

## 🏆 How to Win

- 🎯 Destroy **all the tanks** on the screen to complete the level.
- 🚀 Progress through modes.
- 💯 Try to get the highest score possible!

---

## ❌ Game Over

- The game ends if you **get hit by the falling tank** and it falls off the screen.
